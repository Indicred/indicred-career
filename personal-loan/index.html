Frequently asked questions

Is Indicred a bank or lender?

No. Indicred is a loan enquiry and referral platform. It does not itself sanction or disburse loans. Lending decisions are made by the relevant lender.

Does submitting an enquiry guarantee loan approval?

No. Submission is not approval or a formal loan sanction. Each lending institution independently checks eligibility and decides whether to offer a loan.

Are all providers RBI registered?

No such blanket claim should be assumed. Banks and certain non-bank lenders are regulated under different frameworks. Check the relevant lender's regulatory status and official disclosures before applying.

Will I need to complete another application?

If you choose to proceed, the lender may ask you to complete its own application and verification process.

How will my information be used?

Your information is used to process your enquiry and, where you consent, may be shared with relevant lending partners for the purpose described in the consent and privacy policy.
