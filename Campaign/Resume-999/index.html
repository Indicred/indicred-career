The Problem

A Resume Can Look Professional and Still Get Ignored.

Most resumes simply list responsibilities. The problem is that recruiters are looking for relevance, evidence and positioning.

Generic Resumes

The same resume is sent for every role, even when the responsibilities and expectations are completely different.

Responsibility-Heavy

“Responsible for sales” says what you did. It doesn't demonstrate the business impact you created.

Poor Positioning

Your experience may be strong, but the resume doesn't make the connection between your background and your target role.

Weak First Impression

Recruiters often make an initial decision quickly. A cluttered or unclear resume can hide your strongest evidence.
